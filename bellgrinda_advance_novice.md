# BellGrinda Advanced Novice Training Specification

**Version:** 0.1  
**Training Level:** Advanced Novice  
**Duration:** 6 weeks  
**Phases:** 3  
**Phase Duration:** 2 weeks each  
**Training Days:** Monday–Friday  
**Session Duration:** Approximately 40 minutes  
**Default Structure:** 5 exercises × 5 rounds  
**Primary Equipment:** 12 kg kettlebell, optional 16 kg kettlebell  
**Secondary Equipment:** Skipping rope, mat, chair/bench

---

# 1. Advanced Novice Objective

Advanced Novice builds on the Novice foundation by increasing:

- Training density.
- Exercise complexity.
- Unilateral strength.
- Work capacity.
- Push-up difficulty.
- Load progression.
- Conditioning quality.
- Kettlebell control.
- Athletic movement quality.

The goal is not simply to perform more repetitions.

The level introduces more difficult movement patterns and greater exercise-specific load variation.

---

# 2. Phase Structure

## Phase 1 — Adaptation

**Weeks 1–2**

Focus:

- Transition from Novice workload.
- Introduce harder variations.
- Maintain movement quality.
- Begin selective 16 kg use if available.

Target RPE:

**5–7**

---

## Phase 2 — Development

**Weeks 3–4**

Focus:

- Increased density.
- Increased unilateral work.
- Stronger push-up progression.
- More complex kettlebell combinations.
- Reduced unnecessary rest.

Target RPE:

**6–7**

---

## Phase 3 — Consolidation

**Weeks 5–6**

Focus:

- Stable higher workload.
- Improved work capacity.
- Stronger exercise-specific performance.
- Advanced Novice assessment preparation.

Target RPE:

**6–8**

Frequent RPE 9–10 remains undesirable.

---

# 10. Advanced Novice Push-Up Progression

BellGrinda should transition from raw volume toward difficulty.

Potential progression path:

**Standard**
→ **Tempo**
→ **Pause**
→ **Decline**
→ **Close-grip**
→ future harder variations.

BellGrinda should not assume a harder variation is always better.

Selection depends on:

- chest-development goal;
- strength goal;
- wrist/shoulder comfort;
- total pressing volume.

---

# 11. Load Progression Rules

If 16 kg becomes available, BellGrinda evaluates each movement separately.

Likely earlier candidates:

- Goblet squat.
- Deadlift.
- Row.

Later candidates:

- Swing.
- Clean.
- Press.
- Clean and press.

The app should maintain a per-exercise recommended load rather than one global active kettlebell weight.

---

# 12. 12 kg → 16 kg Rep Adjustment

When moving an exercise to 16 kg:

Initial rep reduction should generally be approximately:

**20–35%**

depending on the exercise.

Examples:

Goblet squat:
15 reps at 12 kg
→
10–12 reps at 16 kg.

Row:
12 reps at 12 kg
→
8–10 reps at 16 kg.

Press:
8 reps at 12 kg
→
4–6 reps at 16 kg if the transition is appropriate.

---

# 13. Density Progression

Advanced Novice begins to use density as a deliberate progression tool.

BellGrinda may reduce rest when:

- prescribed work is completed;
- RPE remains ≤7;
- technique remains stable;
- there is no relevant pain.

Rest reduction should be small.

Example:

60 seconds
→
50 seconds
→
45 seconds

not:

60 seconds
→
20 seconds.

---

# 14. Exercise Difficulty Progression

BellGrinda may progress an exercise when:

- upper rep range is repeatedly completed;
- RPE remains controlled;
- exercise technique remains stable;
- pain flags are absent.

Possible progression:

Goblet squat
→ heavier goblet squat.

Standard push-up
→ tempo push-up.

Reverse lunge
→ loaded reverse lunge.

Dead bug
→ more difficult trunk-control variation.

---

# 15. Regression Rules

BellGrinda may regress a movement if:

- prescribed reps repeatedly fail;
- RPE consistently reaches 9+;
- technique quality falls;
- pain/discomfort occurs;
- workload conflicts with football competition.

Regression can mean:

- fewer reps;
- lighter load;
- easier variation;
- longer rest;
- approved substitute.

Regression is not treated as failure.

---

# 16. Pain Rules

Pain/discomfort logic remains authoritative over progression.

Repeated mild discomfort:

- flag movement;
- maintain or regress.

Moderate discomfort:

- stop exercise for session.

Severe discomfort:

- stop exercise and recommend professional evaluation.

Swing progression remains especially conservative due to previous lower-back discomfort.

# 18. Advanced Novice Performance Test

At the end of Week 6, BellGrinda offers an optional assessment.

## Test 1 — Maximum Clean Push-Ups

Record:

- reps;
- variation used;
- RPE;
- improvement from Novice completion.

---

## Test 2 — Goblet Squat

If still using 12 kg:

**25 controlled repetitions**

If using 16 kg:

**15 controlled repetitions**

Record:

- completion;
- RPE;
- technique.

---

## Test 3 — One-Arm Row

12 kg:
**15 per arm**

or

16 kg:
**10 per arm**

Record:

- completion;
- control;
- RPE.

---

## Test 4 — Press Capacity

12 kg:

**8 controlled reps per arm**

or equivalent current programmed load.

No max-effort grinding.

---

## Test 5 — Conditioning Circuit

Three rounds:

1. Goblet squat — 12.
2. Push-up — 10.
3. Swing — 12.
4. Row — 8 per arm.
5. Skipping — 60.

Record:

- completion time;
- RPE;
- rest taken;
- movement quality.

If swings remain flagged, substitute approved hinge movement.

# 20. Repeat Phase 3 Logic

If the user is dissatisfied with the assessment:

Repeat Advanced Novice Phase 3.

BellGrinda identifies the weakest one or two categories.

Potential reinforcement focuses:

- Upper-body strength.
- Push-up strength.
- Conditioning.
- Lower-body strength.
- Kettlebell technique.
- Training consistency.

After another two weeks:

Performance Test becomes available again.

---

# 21. Advanced Novice Completion Criteria

The level should be considered successful if the user demonstrates most of the following:

- Consistent five-day training.
- Improved work capacity.
- Better control at moderate fatigue.
- Stronger push-up performance.
- Successful harder push-up variation.
- Greater kettlebell load tolerance.
- Improved unilateral strength.
- Controlled use of simple complexes.
- Better conditioning.
- Low incidence of concerning discomfort.

# 23. Transition to Intermediate

Intermediate should introduce more meaningful changes rather than simply increasing every number.

Expected Intermediate changes include:

- More deliberate unilateral loading.
- Greater use of complexes.
- Stronger density progression.
- Greater load differentiation.
- More advanced conditioning structures.
- Stronger push-up variations.
- More exercise-specific progression.
- Potential mixed 12 kg / 16 kg programming in the same workout.
- More explicit recovery management.

Intermediate should remain compatible with the 40-minute BellGrinda workout model.
